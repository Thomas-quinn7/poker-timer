# Chip enquiry: Lion Games

Draft email to [Lion Games](https://www.liongames.eu/chips/) (info@liongames.net),
the recommended chip supplier in [`KIT_SOURCING.md`](KIT_SOURCING.md).

**Why this email exists.** Lion Games sell chips in rolls of 25 at €3.95,
which makes 500 chips in a colour mix of our own choosing about €99 landed.
That is the cheapest route that stops a member buying a matching set off
Amazon, because a mix we picked is not a product anyone can reorder. Two things
are missing from their site and both could move the decision:

1. **Chip weight and material.** At €0.158 a chip these may be light plastic
   rather than clay composite. If they are, the better buy is Poker Merchant at
   €159.95 for 11 g clay composite.
2. **Shipping to Ireland.** Not published. The €20 in the costing is an
   estimate.

Diameter matters too, so the chips stack with anything the society already has.

---

## The draft

**Subject:** Chip enquiry for a college poker society in Dublin

To whom this may concern,

I am Thomas Quinn and I run the poker society in University College Dublin. We
are buying chips for our tournaments and I have been looking at your High
Roller chips in rolls of 25, which look like a very good fit. There are a few
things I could not find on the site however, and I would be very grateful if
you could help me with them.

Could you tell me the weight, the material and the diameter of the High Roller
chips? They will be handled very heavily across a season of tournaments and I
would like to be sure they are not too light before we order.

Could you also confirm that we are able to mix the rolls freely? We would like
to choose our own denominations rather than take a standard set, so something
along the lines of four rolls each of light blue 50, black 100, purple 500,
yellow 1000 and pink 5000. If you could let me know whether you have roughly
twenty rolls of those in stock at the moment that would be very helpful.

Finally, could I get the shipping cost and the delivery time to Dublin in
Ireland, and the price of your deluxe case for 500 chips?

Thank you very much for your time.

Kind regards,
Thomas Quinn

---

## Before sending

- The college name is assumed. Change it if the society is not UCD's.
- The denominations are an example that comes to 20 rolls and 500 chips. Swap
  them for whatever suits the blind structure, keeping the odd colours at the
  top end, since those are the ones worth smuggling in.

## What the answers decide

| If they say | Then |
|---|---|
| Clay composite, 11 g or more, shipping under about €25 | Order the rolls. About €99, three to six days, done. |
| Light plastic, or shipping over about €40 | Switch to Poker Merchant single chips at €0.28, €159.95 all in, 11 g clay composite with the specs published. |
| Rolls cannot be mixed | The whole point is lost. Go to Poker Merchant. |
| 39 mm | Good, matches most existing chips. |
| 40 mm | Fine on its own, will not stack neatly with 39 mm chips the society already owns. |
